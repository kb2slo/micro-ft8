The app is not installed yet

This is normal on a new sidekick. The firmware carries only the setup pages; the app itself is downloaded once, through your phone, and needs the internet.

  1. Put the sidekick on your home WiFi (2.4 GHz only): join its own network and open the setup page, pick your network and save.
  2. Join the same WiFi on your phone and open micro-ft8.local/update.
  3. Press the sidekick's button when asked, then tap Install.

Already on your home WiFi? Go straight to step 2.
