Micro-FT8 sidekick

First-time setup. The app is not on this sidekick yet, and installing it needs the internet.

Install the app over your phone's cellular data →

Stay on this network. Your phone downloads the app over its own data connection and copies it here. An iPhone does this on its own; an Android phone may need to be told to keep using mobile data. If it cannot reach the internet, use your home WiFi instead.

or

Pick your home WiFi below and save. When the sidekick restarts, join the same WiFi on your phone, open http://micro-ft8.local/update, press the sidekick's button and tap Install. After that it can run on its own, with no network.

Operate in AP mode →

Use this sidekick on its own — no home network needed. This is the normal way to run it in the field.

or

Join this device to your WiFi. 2.4 GHz only — this radio cannot see 5 GHz networks. An iPhone Personal Hotspot needs Maximize Compatibility switched on before it will appear.
