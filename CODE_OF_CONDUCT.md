# Code of conduct

Micro-FT8 follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and every other project space, and to anyone acting for the
project elsewhere.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and accept that
the maintainer makes the final call. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private
information are not accepted, here or on the air.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, Jeff Kalikstein KB2SLO, through the contact details on his
[QRZ.com page](https://www.qrz.com/db/KB2SLO). Say what happened, where, and when, with links if you have
them. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer enforces this code using the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
