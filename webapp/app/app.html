Sidekick's own network

The network the sidekick raises when it has no WiFi to join. A password keeps phones that are only looking for internet from joining it. Saving restarts it, and a phone on it must rejoin. Whatever you name it, the app is at micro-ft8.local or 192.168.4.1.

Pairing token

The key this browser sends to change the sidekick. Each address (micro-ft8.local, 192.168.x.x) keeps its own copy, so to pair another address without the button, copy the token from one and paste it into the other. 32 characters, 0-9 and a-f.

Amplifier

For an external amplifier keyed from the QMX's PTT output. With no amplifier, leave both as they are.

The radio keys, then waits this long before sending RF, so the amp's relay can switch first and the QMX's SWR protection doesn't trip on the switching. No amp: 0 works, and the default 50 does no harm. 0 to 500 ms. A reply started late waits the full delay and loses the tones that pass in the meantime.

An amplifier that picks its filter from the RF it senses can trip the QMX's SWR protection on the first transmission on a new band, and the QMX then sends nothing until it is cleared. On: the main board clears the lock and sends that message again in its next slot. A second trip in a row ends your CQ and cancels your QSOs until you call CQ, reply or press Tune. Needs QMX firmware 1_04_004 or later.
