| qauk | <a id="qauk"></a>AP decoding during a QSO | Written from the QEX paper (Franke, Somerville, Taylor 2020), not WSJT-X's code. In a QSO, autoseq knows most of the next message: our call, the partner's call, the expected report, RR73 or 73. Fixing those bits before LDPC is where WSJT-X gains several dB, on the weak replies that finish a QSO. It applies only within a few Hz of the partner's offset (WSJT-X's `napwid` is 50 Hz; pre-filled band-wide, noise decodes as RR73), so it adds a few LDPC tries. Band-wide "CQ ???" and "MyCall ???" are a later step, timed separately. The corpora have no QSO context, so the test is new. **Open:** whether the decode list marks AP decodes, as WSJT-X does. **Done-when:** a host test of synthetic QSO exchanges at falling SNR, partner supplied, gives the gain in dB and the false-decode rate on noise and on a wrong partner; P4 decode time measured; a QSO completes on a decode the SLOT line counts as AP (field). |
| kkap | <a id="kkap"></a>Decode: subtract decoded stations from the audio, around the QSO partner first | On a crowded slot we decode 6% of the stations within 30 Hz of a stronger one, against 90% in clear spectrum ([corpus](https://github.com/kb2slo/micro-ft8/releases/tag/corpus-2026-11-02)); lowering a decoded message's waterfall bins ([decode.md](architecture/decode.md), **Subtraction**) erases what shares them. Re-synthesise each decode, fit its time, frequency, amplitude and phase on the slot's audio, subtract, re-search: from the QEX paper's description, not WSJT-X's code. **First slice:** in a QSO, only the decoded stations overlapping the partner's 50 Hz, then a search there again, the partner first as epic [u8mc](roadmap/several-channels.md)'s order puts it; band-wide is the later slice. **Done-when:** on a host corpus, the share of busy-slot decodes within 30 Hz of a stronger one, measured; if it earns the slice, synthetic replies beside a strong station decode more often, host-measured, and the P4 cost is measured. |
| B80 | <a id="B80"></a>Tools: per-band conditions replace the space-weather card | The card shows raw NOAA numbers, which the operator does not use (maintainer, 2026-11-13); what is wanted is hamqsl.com's per-band Good/Fair/Poor. No public formula exists (N0NBH's is undocumented), but hamqsl's `solarxml.php` carries a computed `<calculatedconditions>` block (80–40, 30–20, 17–15, 12–10 m, day and night). It has no CORS header, so a Worker route fetches it and re-serves that block as small JSON, cached at the Worker so hamqsl sees one request an interval however many stations ask: it is one volunteer's free service. The NOAA fetches and their numbers go. **Open:** hamqsl's terms for reuse, and the credit it asks. **Done-when:** the route serves the block from its cache, the interval chosen from how often the source changes; the card is the per-band table with its source credited, and says so when the route is unreachable; no NOAA request remains; [webapp.md](architecture/webapp.md) (**Tools**) and the site updated. |
| ujtv | <a id="ujtv"></a>Sidekick: a log page cut short says so | `GET /api/log/entries?take=40` answered 16 rows and nothing more (2026-11-10, a 42-QSO day). Two limits cut a page with no sign: the sidekick's reply buffer (`FILE_ENTRIES_JSON_MAX` in `host_link.c`, 4 KB, about 18 rows) drops the rows past it, and the main board's Port A queue (48 frames, `porta_out_queue.h`) drops a large page's oldest, so a page of 50 also loses its first. Download ADI asked for 50 and stopped at about 18 QSOs a day until it asked for 10 (2026-11-26). **Done-when:** a host test shows a reply either holds `take` rows or says where it stopped, for any `take` up to 255. |
| tuj3 | <a id="tuj3"></a>Web app: a main board restart clears what the page was holding | Found on the bench 2026-11-14. After two SWR trips the radio bar's reason read "SWR protection"; the main board was then restarted, came up idle with nothing to report, and the page kept showing the reason until it was reloaded. The page replaces what it holds only when an event arrives, and a restarted board that is idle sends none. **Fix:** the page learns the main board restarted (the sidekick sees its HELLO) and reads `/api/state` again, as a page that has just loaded does. It is the resync a page that fell behind the sidekick's event ring already does ([sidekick.md](architecture/sidekick.md), **Port A**). **Done-when:** a host test shows a page holding a transmit reason and a queue row drop both when told the main board restarted; with the reason showing, restart the main board: it clears with no reload (field). |
| zybt | <a id="zybt"></a>QMX: the link-up queries go unanswered on a reconnect | Found on the bench 2026-11-14, QMX 1_04_010: with the main board left running, a QMX switched off and on leaves the first `SR;` and the `Q9;` read-back unanswered (three reconnects of three), though both are answered on the first connection after the main board boots. `SR;` is now asked again and answered the second time ([radio.md](architecture/radio.md), **SWR protection trips on a QMX**); `Q9;` is asked once, so each reconnect logs "nothing will decode" while decoding works. The cause is not known. Also seen twice that night, 2 and 8 s into a transmission that carried on: `cdc_acm: TX transfer timeout`. **Done-when:** the CAT bytes sent and received in the first two seconds of a reconnect are captured and say why the first query is lost; the `Q9;` warning is true when it shows; the timeout is explained or shown harmless (field). |
| f8u2 | <a id="f8u2"></a>Known issue: after a P4 restart the QMX may not enumerate until switched off and on | Bench, 2026-11-22, QMX 1_04_010: after a P4 restart its USB host fails the QMX (`ENUM: CHECK_SHORT_DEV_DESC FAILED`, no USB_STATUS event) until the QMX is switched off and on. Seen after resets from the ROM bootloader (`a4483c1`) and after the plain restart that ends an over-the-air install (`630ed51`), so a main-board update meets it; `install_firmware.py` warns of it. **Workaround:** switch the QMX off and on. Opening the PROG console restarts the P4 too. Not known: every restart or some; which side cuts the descriptor short. Related: [zybt](#zybt). **Done-when:** the cause named; a restart leaves the QMX connected, or the radio bar says to switch it off and on. |

| znhm | <a id="znhm"></a>Tools: band conditions from live spots | Beside the space-weather card ([B80](#B80) stands).
