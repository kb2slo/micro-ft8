| qauk | <a id="qauk"></a>AP decoding during a QSO | [RFC 0006](rfcs/0006-decode-performance.md) §5 P5 item 3, written from the QEX paper (Franke, Somerville, Taylor 2020), not WSJT-X's code. Fixing those bits before LDPC is where WSJT-X gains several dB, on the weak replies that finish a QSO. The corpora have no QSO context, so the test is new. **Open:** whether the decode list marks AP decodes, as WSJT-X does. **Done-when:** a host test of synthetic QSO exchanges at falling SNR, partner supplied, gives the gain in dB and the false-decode rate on noise and on a wrong partner; P4 decode time measured; a QSO completes on a decode the SLOT line counts as AP (field). |
