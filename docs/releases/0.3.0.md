# 0.3.0

## New
- The QSOs tab syncs your log with your QRZ logbook. Add your QRZ API key in Settings › Station; with the switch on, new QSOs go up by themselves, or tap Send.
- Decodes › Filter has Hide worked, to hide stations already in your log.
- The station stops a transmission that runs too long, and a QSO that keeps sending without an answer.
- Tune stops by itself when the SWR reads over 3.0.

## Changed
- Nothing transmits until Settings › Station has a callsign: CQ, replies, Tune and the SWR sweep say "No callsign set".
- Each sidekick's network has its own default name, micro-ft8.local and four characters from its address, so two in range no longer look like one. If you kept the default, rejoin it after updating.
- Up to eight phones and laptops can join the sidekick's network at once, not two.
- A slow phone or laptop on the network no longer stalls the app for everyone else.
- A new QSO starts on the TX offset where the other station last heard you.
- Sort and Filter on Decodes each open a panel under the bar.
- The slot bar's signal count says the level it counts from, for example "≈9 at ≥ -16 dB".
- A factory reset keeps the app, and the app opens once the sidekick is back.
- A QMX+ is logged as QMX+.
- Choosing the HL2 as the radio takes effect at once.
