# 0.4.0

## New
- Call CQ in FT4. While a band listens to both modes, the FT8 ⇄ / FT4 ⇄ button before Call CQ chooses which it calls in.
- The radio drawer's FT8 and FT4 chips choose what each band listens to, one or both. FT4 alone works on any band, 15 m and 10 m included.
- With both modes listened to, Operate has a slot bar for each, and Decodes › Filter can show Both, FT8 or FT4.
- While you transmit in one mode, the other mode's slot bar reads RX muted for the slots your transmission costs it.
- What you send shows among the decodes on Operate, as a line marked Sending, Sent, Stopped or Not sent.
- Report what this station hears, in Settings › Station, spots the stations you decode on PSKReporter under your call and grid. It is off until you turn it on.
- On an iPhone, the app added to the Home Screen opens full screen with its own icon and name, micro-FT8. Safari shows a hint once.
- With an HL2, the radio drawer shows its gateware version, and says so when micro-FT8 was not tested on it.

## Changed
- Your QRZ key, Send new QSOs to QRZ, the distance unit, the two decode-row switches and the CQ mode are now kept on the station, for every phone. Set them again after updating.
- After Save and join, the link on the Saved page opens the app on your home WiFi still paired.
- The report you send comes from the other station's latest message, as in WSJT-X, so a report sent again follows their latest signal.
- With the radio's dial turned off the station's frequency, CQ and replies say "Radio not on 14.074 MHz" and nothing is sent; after 2 s the CQ ends and queued QSOs are parked.
- If the main board restarts while the app is open, the app sets its clock at once rather than within 3 minutes, so decodes start sooner.
- The app is named micro-FT8.

## Removed
- Settings' Also decode FT4, and the Decodes bar's mode chip: use the FT4 chip in the radio drawer. After updating, each band listens to FT8 alone until you choose.
